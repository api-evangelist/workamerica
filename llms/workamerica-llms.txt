# WorkAmerica

> WorkAmerica was a skilled-labor hiring marketplace that connected job seekers,
> employers, and educators — "connecting education to employment, exclusively for
> the skilled labor force" — with a mission of closing the skills gap. It was a
> 500 Global (500 Startups) portfolio company. The company appears defunct: its
> domain workamerica.co expired (a registrar suspension page was captured by the
> Wayback Machine in December 2018) and as of July 2026 the domain 301-redirects
> to an unrelated Indonesian website (legazy.co.id).

This profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network as a venture-portfolio
lead record. No public API, developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable
artifacts were found for this company.
